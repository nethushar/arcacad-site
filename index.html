Classes

  • Cambridge IGCSE Computer Science and ICT classes: Individual and group classes in Sri Lanka for students taking Cambridge IGCSE Computer Science or ICT. Theory, programming and exam technique are taught together, so students can explain an answer instead of just recalling it.
  • Edexcel IGCSE Computer Science and ICT classes: Individual and group classes in Sri Lanka for students taking Edexcel International GCSE Computer Science or ICT. Students code in Python every week and practise both the written and the on-screen papers.
  • Cambridge AS and A Level Computer Science classes: Individual and group classes in Sri Lanka for Cambridge AS and A Level Computer Science. We build the programming depth and the exam writing that top grades depend on.
  • Edexcel IAS and IAL Computer Science and IT classes: Individual and group classes in Sri Lanka for Edexcel International Advanced Subsidiary (IAS) and International Advanced Level (IAL) students. Clear explanations, plenty of practice, and exam answers that earn their marks.
  • Grade 8 and 9 ICT and Computer Science classes: A friendly foundation class in Sri Lanka for the years before IGCSE. Students learn to think like programmers, work with data and use digital tools with confidence.

Questions parents ask

Which classes do you run?

Cambridge IGCSE, Edexcel International GCSE, Cambridge AS and A Level, Edexcel IAS and IAL, and a Grade 8 and 9 foundation class that gets students ready for IGCSE. All of them run as individual or group classes.

Do you run workshops and trainings for schools, universities and companies?

Yes. ArcAcad runs skill based trainings and workshops for schools, university students and enterprises. Contact us with your institution, the topic and the number of participants, and we will reply with the options.

Do you teach both Edexcel and Cambridge?

Both. Your child's school decides the curriculum, and we teach to that one, so the examples, the wording and the past papers match what they will actually sit.

When is the best time to start?

Grade 8 or 9, if you can. The foundation class builds good programming and logic habits before IGCSE begins. If your child is already part-way through IGCSE or A Level, that is fine too. We start by finding the gaps and work from there.

Which programming language do you use?

Mostly Python. We also teach Cambridge pseudocode, because Cambridge students need to read and write it in the exam.

What do the classes cost?

It depends on the level and the group. Send us an enquiry and we will reply with the options and a quote.

Who teaches the classes?

ArcAcad was founded in 2017 by an industry expert who has taught IGCSE, O-Level and IAL students for close to a decade.
